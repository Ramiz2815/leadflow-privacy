Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

LeadFlow Automation ("we", "us", "our") provides an Instagram messaging and lead-management automation service ("the Service") for business accounts. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through Instagram's official API, how we use it, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your Instagram Professional account to the Service, we may access:

  • Instagram direct messages sent to your connected account
  • Comments made on your connected account's posts
  • Basic Instagram account information (e.g. account ID, username)
  • Message metadata such as timestamps and sender IDs

2. How We Use Information

Information collected is used solely to:

  • Automatically respond to comments and direct messages on your behalf
  • Extract and organize lead information (e.g. name, interest, budget, intent)
  • Display leads in your connected dashboard or spreadsheet
  • Send follow-up messages according to rules you configure

We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens and application secrets are stored securely in server-side environment configuration and are never exposed publicly. Lead data extracted from conversations is stored in the destination you configure (such as a Google Sheet or database) and is accessible only to you and your authorized team members.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your Instagram data with third parties, except where required to operate the Service itself (for example, sending message content to an AI model provider for the sole purpose of extracting lead information), or where required by law.

5. Data Retention

Message content is processed to extract lead information and is not retained longer than necessary for that purpose. Extracted lead data is retained in your chosen storage location until you delete it.

6. Your Rights

You may disconnect your Instagram account from the Service at any time through your Meta app settings. You may request deletion of any data associated with your account by contacting us using the details below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
